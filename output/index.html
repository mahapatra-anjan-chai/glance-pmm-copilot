AI Chatbots Convert 40-50% Better Than Every Other Channel During Prime Day 2026
Adobe Analytics / Forbes / CMSWire ↗

This is the D0 commerce thesis playing out at scale: shoppers who arrive pre-decided buy more. Glance's lockscreen discovery drives the same pre-intent state — users who see a product at wake and tap through are already in purchase mode. The Prime Day data proves ambient pre-intent is now the highest-converting channel in commerce.

Google Launches Universal Commerce Protocol and Universal Cart Across Search, Gemini, YouTube, Gmail
Google Blog / Search Engine Land / Shopify Engineering ↗
Google's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) — developed with Shopify, Target, Walmart, Wayfair, and 20+ partners — went live post-Google Marketing Live, enabling one-tap agentic checkout directly inside AI Mode in Search and Gemini. The Universal Cart lets users accumulate products across Google surfaces (Search, YouTube, Gmail) and check out via Google Pay or transfer to the merchant site. UCP is expanding from the US to Canada, Australia, and the UK, and into hotel booking and local food delivery verticals.
Google's Universal Cart is the walled-garden answer to ambient commerce.
