🕳 Whitespace Angles — Zero coverage in past 28 days

  • Google Universal Cart owns the rails — but still requires an app or browser session open
    Every publication this week covered Google I/O's Universal Cart (spanning Search, Gemini, YouTube, Gmail) as the definitive agentic commerce moment. Zero coverage of the upstream gap: Universal Cart is useless when the phone is locked. Glance operates at D0 — the moment before any Google surface exists — giving it a structural position no protocol (UCP, ACP) can yet address.
    The narrative Glance should own: 'Universal Cart is the best checkout experience inside the walled garden. Glance is the discovery layer that exists before any wall appears.'
  • ChatGPT ads rollout and the monetization compression event
    The AI Ad Economy's top post ('The AI Monetization Shift') and Stratechery's Eric Seufert interview both frame ChatGPT's $2.5B ad revenue trajectory as the defining monetization story of 2026. Coverage universally focuses on conversation-based ad placement inside open apps. No one is discussing the zero-session ad surface: Glance's lockscreen delivers brand impressions and product discovery before a user ever opens ChatGPT, Google, or any other monetizing platform.
    Glance can position itself as the ad reach layer that exists upstream of all conversation-based AI ad inventory — brand-safe, non-interruptive, and structurally upstream of the trust-eroding paid-recommendation model.
  • Consumer trust gap blocking agentic checkout autonomy
    Multiple sources (Forrester, Checkout.com, eMarketer) note that 65.5% of U.S. consumers still distrust agent-led payments and that OpenAI scaled back Instant Checkout after merchant pushback on data control. Coverage frames this as a trust/protocol problem to be solved. No publication has connected this to the OEM trust layer: Glance is pre-installed by Samsung and other OEMs — it carries native device trust, not third-party app trust.
    Glance Zapps inherit OEM credibility in a way that ChatGPT or Klarna apps cannot. This is a differentiated trust story no competitor can replicate.
  • Virtual try-on as a conversion and returns reducer
    Multiple sources including Modern Retail and fashion tech outlets cite virtual try-on driving 40% fewer returns and 25% higher conversion. All coverage assumes the try-on experience lives inside an open app (Nike, Sephora, Warby Parker). Zero coverage of try-on at the lockscreen discovery moment. Glance's Physical AI Stylist — which works without requiring a selfie mid-session — is the only try-on experience that operates at D0 product discovery.
    The angle: try-on has always lived inside the funnel; Glance moves it to the top of the funnel before brand consideration has even formed.
  • Klarna + Google BNPL integration into agentic checkout flows
    Digital Commerce 360 and eMarketer both covered Klarna's May 2026 integration into Google's agentic payments flow and its ChatGPT shopping app launch. Coverage frames BNPL as the financing layer completing agentic transactions. No one has connected BNPL to the discovery moment: if Glance surfaces a product on the lockscreen with an embedded Zapp that includes Klarna financing at point-of-impulse, the impulse-to-purchase window collapses from days to seconds.
    This is a 'BNPL at D0' story no one has told — and Glance is the only entity positioned to tell it with a live product.
  • Agentic commerce protocol wars (UCP vs. ACP) and merchant readiness
    The Agentic Commerce Frontier's two posts this fortnight were both infrastructure-deep: UCP standardizing product offers, AP2 standardizing payment authorization, Shopify's Agentic Storefronts terms, Visa's Agentic Ready program. All coverage focuses on how merchants connect to AI agents through open protocols. The missing angle: Brand AI Store on Glance is a first-party OEM storefront that gives brands a curated, pre-protocol distribution channel — no UCP/ACP integration required to reach 175M lockscreens.
    Glance is the merchant readiness story that doesn't require merchants to build anything new.
