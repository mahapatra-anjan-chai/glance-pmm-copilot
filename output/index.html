Google Launches Universal Cart and Expands Universal Commerce Protocol at I/O 2026
Google Blog / The Next Web / eMarketer ↗
At Google I/O 2026, Google unveiled Universal Cart — an AI-powered shopping basket that persists across Search, Gemini, YouTube, and Gmail, powered by its Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP). Once a product is added, Gemini agents work in the background to monitor price drops, surface deals, and alert on restocks. TechCrunch / Opascope / Efficiently Connected / Commercetools ↗
